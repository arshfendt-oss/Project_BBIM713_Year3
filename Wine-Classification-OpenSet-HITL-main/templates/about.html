About the Capstone Project

Unknown Rejection and Clustering for Post-Training Labelling

Business Context

Large wine databases continuously receive records belonging to both existing and previously unseen Wine Keys. Conventional closed-set classifiers may incorrectly force unseen records into known categories.

Project Objective

  1. Classify known wine records.
  2. Detect unknown or unseen Wine Keys.
  3. Semantically cluster rejected records.
  4. Support human-assisted labelling.
  5. Experimentally retrain the classifier with newly verified records.
